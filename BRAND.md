# Nexum Corpus public-surface system

## Position

**Nexum Corpus builds instruments for verifiable machine work.**

The portfolio should sound exact, calm, and consequential. Lead with the problem a project solves, follow with evidence, and expose limitations before they become objections.

## Visual grammar

- Base: obsidian `#070A12` and slate `#101827`
- Type: system sans for names; system monospace for labels and evidence
- Geometry: grids, orbital rings, measured intersections, sparse signal points
- Accent colors identify projects; they do not change the shared chassis
- No stock AI imagery, robot heads, glowing brains, or unsupported superlatives

## Copy grammar

Every repository surface should answer, in order:

1. What is this?
2. Why does it matter?
3. What can I do in five minutes?
4. What evidence supports the claim?
5. What does it not claim?
6. Where does it sit in the estate?

Use verbs and observable outcomes. Prefer “detects semantic merge risk” to “revolutionizes collaboration.” Prefer “the holdout rejected every winner” to “state-of-the-art discovery.”
