the point, not a caveat. The scarce skill demonstrated in these repositories
is not typing; it is making machine work *verifiable*: generator ≠ grader,
pre-registered experiments, claims certified by gates that can — and do —

## The method

1. **The gate can refuse its maker.** The claims ledger's most recent entry
   is a rejection of our own work. A grader that cannot say no certifies
   nothing.
2. **Pre-registration before results.** Predictions are frozen before the
   sweep runs; null results enter the record as nulls.
3. **Honest ledgers.** Failures, errata, and retractions are first-class
   records, cross-checked by executable drift assertions — not prose.
4. **Overfit is the default fate of winners.** Nothing counts until it
   survives a holdout it has never seen.
